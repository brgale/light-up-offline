How to play

Place bulbs so every white square is illuminated. A bulb shines along its row and column until a dark wall blocks it. Bulbs cannot shine on one another. A number on a wall tells you how many bulbs touch it edge-to-edge.

Choose Bulb, Mark (×), or Erase above. Tap or drag to apply that action to one or more squares. Use Undo to reverse your last tap or drag, and Check when you think you have solved it.

A self-contained game. No account, ads, or internet connection needed.
