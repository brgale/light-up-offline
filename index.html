How to play

Place bulbs so every white square is illuminated. A bulb shines along its row and column until a dark wall blocks it. Bulbs cannot shine on one another. A number on a wall tells you how many bulbs touch it edge-to-edge.

With Bulb selected, tap to cycle through bulb, ×, and empty. Choose Mark or Erase to apply that action, or drag to paint with the selected action. Right-click or drag with the right button to mark squares ×; start on a marked square to erase marks. Use ↔ to swipe around a zoomed board, then turn it off to place bulbs. Your current puzzle, marks, timer, and zoom save automatically on this device. Use Undo to reverse your last action, and Check when you think you have solved it. On a keyboard, press B, M, or E to choose an action; use + or − to zoom and ⌘/Ctrl+Z to undo.
